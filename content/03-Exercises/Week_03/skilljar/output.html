# Nutrition Plan for Marathon Training

## Daily Caloric Breakdown (~3500-4000 cal)

**Macros targeting carb-loading:**
- Carbs: 55-60% (~1925-2400 cal)
- Protein: 15-20% (~525-800 cal)
- Fat: 20-25% (~700-1000 cal)

---

## Sample Day Around 2-Hour Morning Run

### **Pre-Run (1-2 hours before)**
- Oatmeal with banana and honey (300-400 cal)
- Black coffee/tea

### **During Run**
- Sports drink or energy gels (200-300 cal)

### **Post-Run Recovery (within 30 min)**
- Smoothie: Greek yogurt, berries, oats, honey (400-500 cal)
- Toast with nut-free spread

### **Mid-Morning Snack**
- Rice cakes with jam or hummus (200 cal)

### **Lunch**
- Pasta with lentil/chickpea bolognese (800-900 cal)
- Side salad, olive oil dressing

### **Afternoon Snack**
- Fruit + seeds (sunflower/pumpkin) (300 cal)
- Whole grain crackers with cheese

### **Dinner**
- Quinoa or rice-based vegetable stir-fry with tofu/paneer (700-800 cal)
- Legumes included

### **Evening**
- Fortified plant milk, banana (150-200 cal)

---

## Key Foods to Prioritize
✓ Pasta, rice, oats, bread, potatoes  
✓ Lentils, chickpeas, beans, tofu, paneer  
✓ Seeds (sunflower, pumpkin, sesame)  
✓ Fruits, whole grains  
✓ Low-fat dairy (if included)

**Hydration:** 3-4L daily, more around training

Consider consulting a sports nutritionist for personalized timing and adjustments.
